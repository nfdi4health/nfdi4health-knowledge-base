Explore learning opportunities for FAIR research data management in clinical, epidemiological and public health research. This catalogue brings together self-paced Knowledge Base modules, existing NFDI4Health training materials and selected resources from other organisations.

Choose a section according to your learning needs:

- **Section I — Self-paced learning modules:** structured learning developed for the Knowledge Base, including adaptations of previous online training.
- **Section II — NFDI4Health training materials:** existing workshop materials and training guidance from the consortium.
- **Section III — External training resources:** additional learning opportunities from the wider research data management community.

## Section I: Self-paced learning modules

Explore sample modules for independent, asynchronous learning. The catalogue includes modules developed specifically for the Knowledge Base and modules adapted from NFDI4Health online training. Both use the **NFDI4Health Knowledge Base Training Module Authoring Template with Guide**. [1–4]

**The cards below are sample catalogue entries under development. Levels and learning times are indicative; module links will be added when the content is ready.**

## Section III: External training resources

The following resources complement NFDI4Health learning materials. They are maintained by their respective providers and cover broader research data management, FAIR implementation and life science topics.

| Resource | Learning focus | Useful for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FAIR Cookbook](https://faircookbook.elixir-europe.org/) | Practical recipes for making and keeping life science data FAIR. | Researchers and data stewards seeking implementation guidance |
| [RDMkit: All training resources](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/all_training_resources) | An overview of training resources linked from RDMkit pages. | Finding learning materials by RDM topic |
| [ELIXIR TeSS](https://tess.elixir-europe.org/) | A searchable catalogue of life science training materials, events and learning paths. | Discovering topic-specific learning opportunities |
| [EMBL-EBI introductory bioinformatics pathway](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/online/courses/introductory-bioinformatics-pathway/) | Self-paced introductory courses on bioinformatics and biological data resources. | Learners building foundational bioinformatics knowledge |
| [Galaxy Training Network](https://training.galaxyproject.org/) | Practical tutorials and learning pathways for data analysis with Galaxy. | Learners seeking hands-on analysis exercises |
| [Train-the-Trainer Concept on Research Data Management](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13927614) | Guidance and teaching approaches for developing RDM training. | Trainers and module authors |
| [FAIR-by-Design Methodology for learning materials](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14711525) | Guidance on designing reusable and maintainable learning materials. | Authors preparing or adapting training content |
