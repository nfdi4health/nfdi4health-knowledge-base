Explore learning opportunities for FAIR research data management in clinical, epidemiological and public health research. This catalogue brings together self-paced Knowledge Base modules, existing NFDI4Health training materials and selected resources from other organisations.

Choose a section according to your learning needs:

- **Section I — Self-paced learning modules:** structured learning developed for the Knowledge Base, including adaptations of previous online training.
- **Section II — NFDI4Health training materials:** existing workshop materials and training guidance from the consortium.
- **Section III — External training resources:** additional learning opportunities from the wider research data management community.

## Section I: Self-paced learning modules

These modules are intended for independent, asynchronous learning: you can work through them at your own pace without attending a scheduled session. They use static learning pages with explanations, practical workflows and supporting resources.

Both types of module follow the **NFDI4Health Knowledge Base Training Module Authoring Template with Guide**. Each module sets out its learning objectives, target audience, difficulty level, estimated learning time and prerequisites, followed by core concepts and practical guidance. Where appropriate, modules also include NFDI4Health services, a use case and self-check questions. Further learning, related lifecycle phases and maintainer information complete the module. [1]

### A. Modules developed specifically for the Knowledge Base

These modules are authored directly as self-paced learning resources, organised around a practical topic or workflow in the NFDI4Health context. The initial proposed topics are listed below. **These are planned catalogue entries; links will be added when the modules are available.**

| Proposed module | Learning focus | Intended audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovering health studies with the Health Study Hub | Find study descriptions and assess their relevance to a research question. | Researchers, students and data stewards |
| Describing studies with NFDI4Health metadata | Understand study metadata and prepare clear, reusable study descriptions. | Data stewards and study coordinators |
| Assessing the quality of health research data | Identify quality dimensions and plan a documented assessment workflow. | Researchers, analysts and data stewards |
| Terminologies and data harmonisation | Understand how consistent terminology and harmonisation support reuse across studies. | Data stewards and researchers |
| Introduction to privacy-preserving analysis | Understand the purpose of distributed analysis and the role of relevant NFDI4Health services. | Researchers, analysts and research IT staff |

Topics are informed by the consortium's training concept and service portfolio. [2, 3]

### B. Modules adapted from NFDI4Health online training

These modules will reuse materials from previous NFDI4Health online training sessions. Slide content will be adapted into explanations and activities that learners can follow independently, with the original sources acknowledged.

The September 2026 training materials provide a starting point for the following modules. **Adaptation into self-paced modules is planned; the entries below do not indicate completed courses.** [4]

| Proposed module | Existing training material to be adapted |
| --- | --- |
| Foundations of biomedical research data management | What is Research Data Management?; Types of sources in Biomedical/NFDI4Health research data; Introduction NFDI4Health |
| The biomedical research data lifecycle | The Biomedical Research Data Lifecycle |
| FAIR principles and NFDI4Health services | FAIR Data Principles & NFDI4Health Services; Services during FAIR data principles; Summary |
| Data management planning | Data Management Planning |
| Organising research data | Data Organisation |
| Sensitive data and privacy | Sensitive Data and Privacy |
| Secure storage, backup and preservation | Data Storage, Backup, Security and Long-Term Preservation in Biomedical Research; Archiving and Long-Term Preservation |

Each adaptation will use the same authoring template as a newly developed module. Existing attribution and licence information will be retained, and examples, service descriptions and links will be reviewed during adaptation. Learning time and difficulty will be assigned to the completed module rather than copied from the duration of the original training session.

<details>
<summary><strong>Guidance for module authors</strong></summary>

<p>Use the <em>NFDI4Health Knowledge Base Training Module Authoring Template with Guide</em> for both module types. The template supports iterative contributions: authors can start with the information available and complete remaining sections during development.</p>

<ul>
<li>State whether the module is newly authored or adapted from an existing training session.</li>
<li>Use the template's Introductory or Intermediate difficulty categories and specify prerequisites.</li>
<li>Write instructions that can be followed without a live trainer. Add explanations for demonstrations and activities originally delivered during a session.</li>
<li>Where self-check questions are included, provide answers or explanatory feedback in the learner-ready version.</li>
<li>Link relevant lifecycle pages and services, and record sources, original authors, licences and adaptations.</li>
<li>Identify the module maintainer and review date. List contributors only where their contribution is confirmed.</li>
</ul>

<p>The shared template and FAIR-by-Design guidance support consistent structure, reuse and maintenance. [1, 5]</p>
</details>

## Section III: External training resources

The following resources complement NFDI4Health learning materials. They are maintained by their respective providers and cover broader research data management, FAIR implementation and life science topics.

| Resource | Learning focus | Useful for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FAIR Cookbook](https://faircookbook.elixir-europe.org/) | Practical recipes for making and keeping life science data FAIR. | Researchers and data stewards seeking implementation guidance |
| [RDMkit: All training resources](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/all_training_resources) | An overview of training resources linked from RDMkit pages. | Finding learning materials by RDM topic |
| [ELIXIR TeSS](https://tess.elixir-europe.org/) | A searchable catalogue of life science training materials, events and learning paths. | Discovering topic-specific learning opportunities |
| [EMBL-EBI introductory bioinformatics pathway](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/online/courses/introductory-bioinformatics-pathway/) | Self-paced introductory courses on bioinformatics and biological data resources. | Learners building foundational bioinformatics knowledge |
| [Galaxy Training Network](https://training.galaxyproject.org/) | Practical tutorials and learning pathways for data analysis with Galaxy. | Learners seeking hands-on analysis exercises |
| [Train-the-Trainer Concept on Research Data Management](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13927614) | Guidance and teaching approaches for developing RDM training. | Trainers and module authors |
| [FAIR-by-Design Methodology for learning materials](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14711525) | Guidance on designing reusable and maintainable learning materials. | Authors preparing or adapting training content |
