# NFDI4Health Training Modules

The NFDI4Health Knowledge Base includes self-paced training modules designed to help researchers, data stewards, and health research professionals build practical competencies in FAIR biomedical research data management.

Each module introduces key concepts, practical guidance, relevant services, and applied examples aligned with the health research data life cycle.
