# Standards for contributor content

These proposed working standards support consistent NFDI4Health Knowledge Base pages. They complement the existing site style guide; the coordination team should approve them as project policy before describing them as mandatory consortium rules.

## Audience and writing

Write for researchers, study coordinators, data stewards and service users working with clinical, epidemiological or public health data. Begin with the user problem, explain the actionable steps, distinguish essential from optional actions and expand acronyms on first use. Keep claims verifiable and version-sensitive.

## Required information by page type

| Page type | Minimum information for editorial intake |
| --- | --- |
| Service | What the service does, intended users, verified service URL/status, access/support route, limitations, relevant FAIR/lifecycle activities, responsible content steward, references. |
| Self-paced module | The **agreed V001 headings 0–15**, explicitly showing unfinished sections where necessary. Objectives, concepts, workflow and self-checks are developed and reviewed before a module is described as complete. |
| Life cycle | Context and purpose, recommended actions, NFDI4Health service connections, biomedical example, safeguards, checklist, references and responsible content steward. |

Do not confuse a directory/listing, a training slide deck or a proposed catalogue entry with a completed, pedagogically reviewed module.

## Evidence and links

Prefer current official NFDI4Health sources, peer-reviewed literature, recognised standards and checked service documentation. Record a link or DOI, title, issuing organisation, date/version where available, and date checked for rapidly changing material. Do not copy substantial third-party material without adequate permission and attribution. Identify illustrative examples explicitly.

## Privacy, ethics and data security

Do **not** add participant-level health data, identifiers, consent forms, institutional credentials, private contact details or restricted screenshots. Use synthetic, fully fictional examples unless explicit approval and legal clearance exist. Check that even metadata or screenshots do not inadvertently reveal an individual. Distinguish access to public metadata from access to protected health data, and never promise that an external data holder will grant access.

## FAIR and NFDI4Health specificity

Show how the guidance affects Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability and/or Reusability only where genuinely relevant. Name a service only when its function is applicable and currently supported; mark pilots and planned capabilities clearly after owner verification. Link between service, module and life-cycle content without duplicating whole pages.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use logical headings, informative link labels and meaningful alternative text for images. Avoid information communicated solely through colour. Write understandable tables, explain specialist vocabulary and proofread English; a German translation may be added when it has a verified maintainer, but should not be assumed to exist.

## Attribution, licensing and maintenance

Record original authors, editors, sources and image licences. The repository's current `LICENSE` states CC BY 4.0 for documentation and MIT for software; inherited RDMkit wording must be reviewed before a broader NFDI4Health rights policy is announced. Request consent before publishing names, emails or ORCID identifiers. Propose a content steward and a review date. For revisions, document what changed and why.

## Submission readiness checklist

- [ ] An appropriate template is used and the existing source URL is noted for updates.
- [ ] Verifiable claims and current service links have references.
- [ ] No personal/restricted health data, secrets or unlicensed materials are included.
- [ ] Published/operational versus under-development claims are clearly differentiated.
- [ ] Images, tables and links are accessible and properly credited.
- [ ] A subject-matter contact and maintenance owner are nominated (not necessarily yet confirmed).
- [ ] Sections still needing completion are explicitly marked.
