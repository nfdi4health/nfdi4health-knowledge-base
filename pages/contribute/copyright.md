# Copyright and attribution

Please contribute original wording or material you have the right to publish. When referring to an external resource, cite and link it; do not assume that material found online is freely reusable.

## Text and quotations

Use your own explanation of concepts and include the source. Keep direct quotations short, exact, clearly marked and attributed. Republishing a chart, text block or document may require separate permission even if you cite it.

## Images, figures and screenshots

For every submitted image, provide its creator, source URL, licence, required attribution and any changes made. Verify that a screenshot does not expose patient details, usernames, tokens or private data. If rights are unclear, replace it with your own cleared diagram or omit it pending review. Include a meaningful image description for accessibility.

## Existing materials and licences

At the time this guidance was drafted, the repository's `LICENSE` describes **CC BY 4.0 for documentation** and **MIT for software**, with exceptions where stated. Some repository text still refers to the original RDMkit project. The coordination team must review those inherited notices and third-party exceptions before announcing a definitive updated NFDI4Health licence policy.

Content owners are responsible for flagging rights issues; an editorial review is required before public release. Being credited as a contributor is distinct from holding exclusive rights over a page. See the [contribution guidelines]({{ '/content_standards' | relative_url }}).
