# Volunteer editorial and review team

NFDI4Health colleagues may volunteer to help maintain a clear, reliable Knowledge Base. The review team supports the coordination team; it does **not** automatically gain permission to publish to the website. The responsibilities and approval rules below are a **proposed operating model for confirmation** by the coordination team.

## Volunteer roles

| Role | Typical review tasks | Can publish? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject-matter reviewer (data steward / service expert) | Accuracy, service status, biomedical workflow, access limitations and references | No, unless separately authorised |
| Editorial reviewer | Clarity, V001/module structure, accessibility, citations, licence attribution and consistency | No, unless separately authorised |
| Training reviewer | Objectives, workflow/example alignment, learning checks and reusable learning structure | No, unless separately authorised |
| Technical reviewer | Markdown, YAML front matter, Liquid links, Jekyll preview and build problems | Only if separately authorised |
| Coordinator / editorial lead | Intake, assignment, decision log, communication and publication recommendation | Only if separately authorised |
| Repository publisher / maintainer | Final PR verification, merge, public-site check and rollback if necessary | Yes, within GitHub permissions |

A person may volunteer for more than one review area. Significant new content should receive **at least one subject check and one editorial check**, ideally from people other than the author. Small factual corrections can use a proportionate review path if approved by the coordinator.

## How review works

1. **Intake:** submission by Word/Markdown or GitHub PR is recorded with its existing page/issue, version and proposed owner.
2. **Triage:** coordinator identifies the content type, urgency, any privacy/rights concerns and reviewers.
3. **Subject review:** service-specific claims, references, intended audience and restricted-data statements are verified.
4. **Editorial / training review:** structure, language, accessibility, attribution, internal links and V001 compliance (modules) are checked.
5. **Author confirmation:** substantive changes are returned to the contributor or service owner for confirmation.
6. **Decision:** accept; accept subject to minor edits; revise and resubmit; or do not publish with a recorded explanation.
7. **Technical publication:** approved content is placed in a branch/PR; checks and final review are completed; an authorised maintainer merges.
8. **Post-publication:** public page and downloads are checked, changes are logged, and review responsibility is assigned.

## Become a reviewer

Contact the Knowledge Base coordination team through the approved NFDI4Health collaboration channel. Indicate your expertise, preferred review types, possible availability and whether you would rather comment in Word or GitHub. You can volunteer without becoming a GitHub maintainer. Access and any public recognition will be discussed before onboarding.

## Independence and transparency

Authors should not be the sole approvers of their own significant new material. A reviewer should declare a conflict of interest or lack of expertise and request reassignment where appropriate. Review comments should focus on evidence and user needs. The team should log major decisions and keep private contact information in an institutionally approved location, not in public source files.

For detailed procedures, coordinators can consult the internal-use **Editorial SOP and checklists** distributed with this package. Those materials do not create an automatically approved NFDI4Health policy.
