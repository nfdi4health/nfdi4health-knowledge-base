title: How to contribute to the NFDI4Health Knowledge Base

This Knowledge Base is built by the NFDI4Health community, and you're welcome to help shape it — whether that's fixing a typo, improving a page, or adding something new.

If you contribute, please remember to:

* Follow our [style guide](style_guide).
* Respect copyright — see our [Copyright guidelines](copyright).
* Credit yourself and others — see [Acknowledgement and ownership of content](#acknowledgement-and-ownership-of-content) below.

## Acknowledgement and ownership of content

Contributors are shown at the bottom of each page and on the main [Contributors page](contributors) if listed in the page's metadata. Add your name, ORCID, email, GitHub handle, and affiliation to the [CONTRIBUTORS file](https://github.com/nfdi4health/nfdi4health-knowledge-base/blob/main/_data/CONTRIBUTORS.yaml).

No single contributor or editor owns this content — it's community-led, with decisions driven by consensus. Content may be revised by others over time; the goal is always accuracy for the community, not ownership of any one version.

Found something that needs fixing? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nfdi4health/nfdi4health-knowledge-base/issues/new/choose).
