## About the NFDI4Health Helpdesk

The **NFDI4Health Helpdesk** is the official contact point for questions about managing personal health research data, using NFDI4Health tools and services, and related organisational matters. It welcomes enquiries from researchers, students, lecturers, data holders and others interested in health research data.

Support is available **free of charge in German and English**. The Helpdesk's data stewards coordinate enquiries and, when specialist knowledge is needed, involve relevant experts within NFDI4Health.

**Need assistance?** [Visit the official NFDI4Health Helpdesk to submit your enquiry](https://www.nfdi4health.de/en/helpdesk.html).

The enquiry form is hosted on the official NFDI4Health website, not within this Knowledge Base. If the form is not visible, check the functional-cookie settings on the official website.
