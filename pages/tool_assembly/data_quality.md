# Data Quality Assessments

## What is this service?

The NFDI4Health Data Quality service supports researchers, data stewards, and health data professionals in assessing and improving the quality of research data used in clinical, epidemiological, and public health studies.

Reliable scientific research depends on high-quality data. However, while substantial effort is often invested in study design and data collection, data quality assessment itself is frequently insufficiently standardised, poorly documented, or difficult to reproduce.

NFDI4Health addresses this challenge by making data quality assessment more accessible through practical concepts, reusable methods, community engagement, and software tools.

The goal is to strengthen transparent, efficient, and FAIR-oriented health research.

## Why use this service?

The Data Quality service helps research teams to:

- identify data inconsistencies
- improve completeness and accuracy
- strengthen reproducibility
- improve transparency of quality assessment methods
- support FAIR data reuse
- detect documentation issues early
- improve trustworthiness of research outputs
- support harmonisation readiness

High-quality data are essential for:

- robust analysis
- valid conclusions
- interoperability
- secondary reuse
- responsible health research

## Governance and Sensitive Data Considerations

Data quality assessment frequently involves sensitive health data.

Important considerations include:

- GDPR compliance
- secure processing
- minimisation principles
- controlled environments
- governance approval where required

Quality assessment should be performed in legally compliant research environments.

## Practical Workflow Example

### Example Scenario: Multicentre Diabetes Study

A multicentre diabetes study is preparing pooled analysis.

**Step 1 — Plan quality checks**

The data steward defines quality assessment criteria.

---

**Step 2 — Assess completeness**

The team evaluates missingness across study variables.

---

**Step 3 — Check plausibility**

Unexpected values and inconsistencies are identified.

---

**Step 4 — Review harmonisation readiness**

Variable structures are assessed before pooling.

---

**Step 5 — Document assessment results**

Methods and findings are documented transparently.

---

**Step 6 — Proceed to analysis**

Only quality-reviewed data are advanced for downstream analysis.
